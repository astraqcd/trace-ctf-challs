# Everyone Knows the Face

**Category:** OSINT · **Difficulty:** medium · **Points:** 100 · **Type:** static

## Description

The most reproduced photograph of the twentieth century. Everyone knows the face; almost nobody knows the day.

Name the photographer, the date the shutter fired, and the city.

**Flag format:** `trace{PhotographerSurname_YYYY-MM-DD_City}`. Give the surname he is credited under, on its own, and the city in its usual English spelling. Use underscores for spaces.
