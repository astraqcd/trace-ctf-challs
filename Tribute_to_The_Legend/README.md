# Tribute to "The Legend"

**Category:** OSINT · **Difficulty:** easy · **Points:** 100 · **Type:** static

## Description

"Giving Up is not in the blood sir." He went from the Gurkhas, to the Special Boat Service, to fourteen summits in six months. He was going round for a third time.

Identify this man, then tell us the highest altitude he reached on his final climb and where he was standing when he reached it.

**Flag format:** `trace{elevation,lat,lon}`. Give the elevation in metres to **exactly 2 decimal places** with no thousands separator, then latitude and longitude in decimal degrees to **exactly 6 decimal places**. Comma separated, no spaces. Example shape: `trace{1234.56,12.345678,98.765432}`.
